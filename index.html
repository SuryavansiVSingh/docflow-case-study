The problem

Small businesses still copy invoices into Excel by hand

It takes hours

Every supplier invoice and receipt is opened, read and typed over. Every month, again.

Mistakes slip through

A total that doesn't match its VAT, the same invoice paid twice, a date typed wrong.

Inboxes are a risk

Fake "unpaid invoice" emails carry zip files and macros. One wrong click is enough.

How it works

Five steps, every few minutes, without anyone clicking

  1. Check the inbox

    Reads new mail over IMAP with an app password. Never marks mail as read, never deletes or sends.

  2. Safety first

    Only PDFs and images are opened. Zip, exe, macros, disguised files and fake senders are blocked.

  3. Read the document

    Fixed Dutch/English rules for normal invoices. AI only for photos and unusual layouts.

  4. Check the numbers

    Net + VAT = total? Real dates? Usual VAT rate? Seen this invoice before?

  5. Deliver

    Excel or Google Sheets, a review list for doubtful items, and a short Slack/Teams message.

The checks

It reads the numbers, not just the text

Every document is checked like a bookkeeper would: do the lines add up, is the VAT rate normal (0, 6, 12 or 21% in Belgium), is the date real, is the due date after the invoice date, was this supplier and invoice number seen before.

This invoice also hides a tiny white line telling "the AI assistant" to approve it. DocFlow treats document text as data, never as instructions, so it lands in review like any other mistake.

Review screen

People only look at what needs a person

The document sits next to the fields DocFlow found. Fix a value, click Save & re-check, and it moves to Ready when the numbers add up. Blocked emails show why they were stopped; their attachments are never opened or saved.

Safety & privacy

Built to be trusted with a mailbox

  • Read-only on the mailbox: no deleting, moving, sending or paying.
  • Runs on the client's own computer or server, with their own accounts and AI key.
  • Passwords and keys live only in a local settings file, never in code.
  • The AI can only fill in fields. Its answer is type-checked; it cannot approve anything.
  • Every email and file is remembered, so nothing is booked twice.
